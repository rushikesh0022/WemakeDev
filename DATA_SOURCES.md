# Prototype product imagery

The storefront uses local, normalized copies of openly accessible dataset images so cards stay fast and do not hotlink third-party servers at runtime.

- [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/) product-front images for packaged groceries. Database contents are available under ODbL; product images remain subject to their individual rights.
- [Grocery Store Dataset](https://github.com/marcusklasson/GroceryStoreDataset) supplies one iconic image for every one of its 81 fine-grained classes. The reproducible importer is `scripts/import_grocery_store_dataset.py`.
- [DummyJSON Products](https://dummyjson.com/products) sample catalog thumbnails for a small set of household products.
- [Recognizing Groceries](https://github.com/PerceptiLabs/Recognizing-Groceries) grocery photographs used as temporary catalog fallbacks.

These assets are suitable for the local prototype. Replace them with licensed supplier or retailer catalog photography before commercial use.
